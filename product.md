# Product

ShiftVireo: Plan staffing around actual service demand.

AI workforce scheduling software that helps care providers forecast administrative and support staffing requirements.

- Founder and CEO: Abubakar Sadiq
- Co-founder and CTO: Tolani Ogundipe
- Industry: Healthcare, Human Resources, Enterprise Software
- Customers: Care homes, outpatient facilities and homecare agencies
- Core features: Workload forecasting, availability management, schedule recommendations, staffing gap alerts, utilization reports
- MVP: Shift records and manager-approved staffing recommendations
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, ECS/Fargate, DynamoDB, S3, Lambda
- Business model: Subscriptions priced by workforce size
- Main action: Request a pilot
