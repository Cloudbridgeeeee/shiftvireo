How a ShiftVireo pilot runs

  1. Send shift records and the current rota

    We load six months of shift records for one site and your support roles.

  2. Read the workload forecast

    Within a week you see forecast support hours for the next 14 days against your rota.

  3. Approve the recommended rota

    Your manager reviews each suggested change, edits what they need and publishes the rota.

ShiftVireo’s scheduling platform on AWS

The platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

  • S3 and Lambda

    Shift records and rota exports stored per provider and checked as they arrive

  • SageMaker AI

    Workload forecasts per role, retrained weekly

  • ECS/Fargate

    The rota service that matches shifts to staff availability

  • DynamoDB

    Staff, shifts, recommendations and the approval log

Questions

What care providers ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

Which staff does it plan for?

Admin and support roles only, never nursing or clinical staff.

Does it publish rotas by itself?

No. A manager approves every recommendation before the rota goes out.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by workforce size. We quote after the pilot.

Which records do you need?

Six months of shift records as CSV or Excel, from your rota tool or spreadsheets.

Does it hold resident data?

No. It uses shift records and activity counts, never resident or patient records.
